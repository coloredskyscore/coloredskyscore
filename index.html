01 · TWO RATINGS, NEVER BLENDED

TradFi asks whether an institution can hold and use the asset today. AI asks whether an autonomous agent can safely transact on the chain today. Each has its own gates, categories and tier names.

02 · GATES CAN CAP A RATING

Three pass, partial or fail checks per axis come first. A partial or failed check sets a ceiling, and a chain marked capped would rate higher without it.

03 · LIVE TODAY, NOT ROADMAP

Testnet features and announced plans earn nothing until they ship to mainnet. Price, sentiment and narrative never move a rating. Read the project constitution →
