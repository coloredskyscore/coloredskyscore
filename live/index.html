HOW IT'S READ

A small collector reads each chain from public node endpoints every two minutes, with a backup endpoint if the first fails. Each track ends at the last successful read, not at "now".

FAST AND SLOW CHAINS

Bitcoin and Ethereum show every block. Solana is too fast to draw block by block, and the XRP Ledger rounds its close times to ten seconds, so both are drawn from block counts per interval.

POSSIBLE HALT

Flagged only when two separate endpoints both show no new block for longer than the chain's limit: 4 minutes for Ethereum, 1 for Solana and XRP, 69 for Bitcoin, a gap with under a 0.1% chance on a healthy network. Every flag is checked by hand before it touches a rating.

DATA UNAVAILABLE

No endpoint answered for seven minutes. That's a problem with our data source, not the chain, and it is never shown as a halt. Finality times for each chain are coming in a later update.
